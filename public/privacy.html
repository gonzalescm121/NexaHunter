Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 25, 2026

Information we process

When you sign in, NexaHunter receives identity information made available by Cloudflare OAuth, such as your account identifier, name, and email address when provided. We use this information to authenticate your session and keep your paper-trading account separate from other users.

Paper-trading data

Your simulated cash balance, positions, and paper orders are stored in Cloudflare Durable Objects associated with your authenticated account identifier.

Market data

NexaHunter may request market data from configured providers. Provider credentials are stored server-side and are not exposed to the browser.

Cookies

We use secure, HTTP-only cookies for short-lived OAuth state and authenticated sessions. OAuth state cookies expire quickly and session cookies expire after eight hours.

Security

We use HTTPS, restrictive security headers, authenticated API routes, input validation, rate limiting, duplicate-order protection, and a paper-only execution boundary.

Contact

For privacy requests, use the support channel provided by the NexaHunter operator.
