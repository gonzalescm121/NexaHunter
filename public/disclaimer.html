Paper Trading & Market Data Disclaimer

NexaHunter is a simulation and market-validation platform. It is not a brokerage and is not a financial adviser.

Simulated execution

Every order is processed as PAPER and recorded as FILLED_PAPER. The production application intentionally contains no live-order endpoint or live-trading toggle.

Market information

Quotes, bars, signals, AI-generated analysis, and other market information can be delayed, incomplete, or incorrect. Data should be independently verified before making any real-world financial decision.

Risk

Past performance and simulated results do not predict future results. Trading and investing involve substantial risk, including loss of capital.
